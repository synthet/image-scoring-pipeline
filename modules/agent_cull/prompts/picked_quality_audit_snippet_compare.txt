Picked-image quality audit (advisory only — pairwise comparison required):
- After deciding rejected images, open EVERY picked thumbnail in picked_image_ids.
- Compare subject sharpness PAIRWISE across all picked images: for each pick, ask whether another picked image shows the same moment/subject with sharper focus on the main subject.
- Flag any picked image that is WORSE on subject focus than another picked alternative for the same subject/moment, even if its score_technical is higher.
- Typical failure: foreground subject soft with sharp background while another pick has the subject in focus.
- For each flagged pick add picked_image_advisories with suggested_alternatives listing the sharper picked id(s).
- ADVISORY ONLY: do NOT change pick status or add picks to rejected_image_decisions.
- Return [] only when no picked image is clearly worse on subject focus than another pick.

picked_image_advisories entry fields:
- image_id, filename, issue (misfocus|blur|exposure|composition|other), confidence 0..1, reason (must cite pairwise comparison), suggested_alternatives (picked ids), risk_flags.
