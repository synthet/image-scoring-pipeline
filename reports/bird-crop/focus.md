Crop separates better than the full frame for **3 of 6** measures.

> **Read the `tracks blur?` column before the AUC column.** A measure that does not fall when detail is destroyed cannot support a claim about focus, however well it separates the two groups — it is separating them for some other reason (scene complexity, subject size, contrast). `local_entropy` is the case in point: blurring a pattern *raises* it (`test_entropy_does_not_track_blur`), so a high AUC from it is a confound, not a focus signal.
